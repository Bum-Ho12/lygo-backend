# Security Policy

Lygo processes confidential and privileged legal data. Please report vulnerabilities privately
to the maintainers (do not open a public issue). Include steps to reproduce and impact.

We aim to acknowledge within 2 business days and to fix critical issues within 7 days.

Incident handling follows the breach runbook: contain, assess, notify affected firms without
delay so they (as controllers) can meet the ODPC 72-hour notification requirement.

See [docs/07-security-and-compliance.md](docs/07-security-and-compliance.md) for controls.
